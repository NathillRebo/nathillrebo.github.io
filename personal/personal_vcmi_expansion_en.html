Heroes of Might and Magic III VCMI The Greatest Expansion

Heroes of Might and Magic III is a turn-based strategy game in which you build cities and recruit heroes to lead your army, capture resources and artifacts on the map, and ultimately defeat all your opponents. VCMI is a project whose goal is to create an open-source version of the game, allowing players to easily add their own content, such as units, artifacts, spells, factions, biomes, etc.

What my mod offers

  • New artifacts
  • New spells
  • New heroes
  • New secondary skills
  • New map objects

Technical details

  • Technical side is purely based on json files.
  • Hero portraits are AI-generated, using a model I trained for that purpose.
