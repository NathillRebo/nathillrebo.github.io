Custom RPG System

A custom RPG system based on d100, adapted to a post-apocalyptic universe with magic. Quite brutal, where every attack on a player is dangerous, but still allows for ‘a little more’.

Included game aspects

  • Multiple options for character creation and development - choose your country of origin and profession, gain random talents from a large pool, develop the attributes and skills you want
  • Unique weapon system - weapons are sets of active abilities that sometimes offer unique possibilities
  • Stress - allows punishing players for rash decisions and wasting ‘resources’ such as health
  • Diseases - a simple but interesting system of diseases that develop or are fought off by the player's body dynamically, rather than as an effect lasting a certain amount of time
  • Easy system for creating and enchanting items - an extensive list for creating items and many bonus options for item enchanting
  • Extensive list of spells
