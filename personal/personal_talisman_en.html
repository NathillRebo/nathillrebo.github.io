Talisman Content Pack

Talisman is a board game in which you control a Character, explore lands, defeat monsters, collect items, and ultimately complete the Warlock's Quest, obtain the Talisman, and reach the center of the board. The first player to do so wins the game.

What I have created

  • New Characters (134 for 4th edition, 90 for 5th edition)
  • New Adventure cards (225 Dungeon cards for 4th edition, 636 Adventure cards for 4th edition, 121 Adventure cards for 5th edition)
  • New Spells (85 for 4th edition, 33 for 5th edition)
  • New Rewards (22 for 4th edition, 88 for 5th edition)
  • New Endings, including game-changing ones (36 for 4th edition)
  • New boards (2 for 5th edition, each with 33 own Adventure cards)
  • New mechanics - alternative spaces, starting bonuses, Corruption cards, alternative Talismans
