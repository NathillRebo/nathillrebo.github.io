Warcraft 3 Custom Maps

Warcraft 3 is a real-time strategy game in which you choose one of four factions, gather an army, build cities, recruit units, and destroy the enemy base. The game stands out for its RPG elements—you can recruit heroes who gain levels and can receive items. Warcraft 3 is particularly known for its map editor, which allows you to easily create scenarios that can be treated as separate games. It was thanks to this editor that DotA was created, which started the entire genre of games.

Below are maps that I created myself and maps created by other players that I have significantly modified.

Random Arena

Author: Ja

Number of players: 2~12

Categories: Arena

Game time: 40~60 minut

Random Arena is a scenario in which we control a random unit, earn points by killing other players, and lose points by dying to neutral enemies. The first player to earn 100 points wins the game.
At the beginning, we control low-level units, but by killing enemies, we can evolve into stronger units and buy items that further strengthen us. Runes also appear sporadically on the map, which can be picked up for various random effects. In addition, every three minutes, a special random event occurs, affecting the game globally.
The goal of the game is not a balanced match, but to be absolutely random, chaotic and fun.

Tree Tag

Author: Ja

Number of players: 4, 8 or 12

Categories: Tag

Game time: ~45 minut

Treetag is a map where players control either a treant or an infernal. The treants must run away and hide from the infernals and, if possible, build a base and army powerful enough to defeat them. The infernals must find and destroy all the treants before they expand and become unbeatable.

Zombination

Author: TheSpoon, Ja

Number of players: 1~10

Categories: Survival, Tag

Game time: Up to ~45 minut

Zombination is a map in which you play as a resident of a city destroyed by an army of zombies, who must survive for 40 minutes until a helicopter arrives, and then get on it to win.
The map offers item craftin, interaction with the environment, and a variety of heroes that you can control.

Differences between this version and the original
  • Zombie Lord is controlled by AI
  • Increased hero pool
  • Hero selection - players choose one out of three random chosen ones
  • Increased pool of craftable items
  • Incresed map size
  • Killing zombies grants points that can be spend on upgrades
  • Random, special zombies with additional abilities
  • Various zombie upgrades that can alter the gameplay
  • On death, heroes become ghosts that can aid their allies despite being dead

Warchasers II

Autor: Cyber_Rebirth, Ja

Ilość graczy: 1~6

Kategorie: Dungeon Crawler

Czas rozgrywki: Do ~45 minut

Warchasers II is a map in which you control a hero and traverse a dungeon, defeating enemies, gaining experience, and collecting items. Along the way, you must also solve puzzles, and the ultimate goal is to defeat the main boss.

Differences between this version and the original
  • Hero pool increased from 16 to 38
  • Max number of players increased to 6
  • Expanded pool of items that can be looted
  • Longer dungeon
  • New bosses
  • New ‘attractions’ such as tank control or base defense
