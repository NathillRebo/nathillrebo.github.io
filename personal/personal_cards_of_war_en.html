Cards of War

Cards of War is a card game in which the goal is to defeat your opponent by playing cards, spells, and upgrades. There are six factions to choose from in the game, each with its own strengths and weaknesses. The game is played on a randomly generated board with strategic points, control of which allows you to gain an advantage over your opponent. The game is designed for an even number of players.

Game contents

  • 240 cards, divided into 6 decks, one for each faction
  • 99 terrain cards
